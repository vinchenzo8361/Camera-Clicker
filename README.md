# 📸 Camera Clicker v1.0.0

A highly polished, feature-rich idle clicker game!

## ✨ Features
* **Exponential Shop:** From standard Auto Flashes to the billion-tier Time-Lapse Matrix.
* **Frenzy Meter:** An active combo mechanic. Click fast enough to fill the volumetric flask and earn a 2x multiplier!
* **Golden Events:** Keep an eye out for random golden cameras that float across the screen for massive rewards.
* **Offline Progress:** The game saves your timestamp and safely rewards you with offline progress when you return.
* **Prestige System:** Reach 1 Trillion photos to buy the Universe Snapshot, beat the game, and earn permanent crowns!
* **Detailed Analytics:** Track your total manual clicks, time played, and coins spent.

Built entirely with HTML, CSS, and Vanilla JavaScript.
